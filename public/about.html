About Me

I'm an aspiring software engineer with a strong interest in building practical, scalable and meaningful software solutions. My journey into technology started with curiosity about how websites and applications work, but that curiosity has grown into a deeper interest in software engineering, problem-solving and understanding the systems behind the applications we use every day.

My primary focus is full-stack web development, with a growing foundation in JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Node.js and Express.js. I've worked with technologies such as MongoDB, and I'm continuously improving my understanding of backend development, databases, APIs and software architecture. I enjoy taking on projects that challenge me to think beyond simply writing code and encourage me to understand why things work the way they do.

I'm a firm believer in continuous learning, consistency and building through experience. From experimenting with small projects to developing more structured applications, every project is an opportunity to strengthen my skills and become a better problem-solver. My long-term goal is to become a well-rounded software engineer capable of designing, developing and delivering reliable software that makes a difference.
